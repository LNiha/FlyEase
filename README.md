It is designed to showcase your ability to move from data-driven problem identification to high-fidelity prototyping—the perfect "Product Analyst meets UX Designer" case study.FlyEase: Streamlining Travel Disruption ManagementFlyEase is a mobile solution designed to assist connecting passengers facing unexpected flight delays and cancellations. By bridging the gap between complex airline regulations and user anxiety, the platform automates the documentation and rebooking process for multi-airline journeys.
📊 The Problem: The "Layover Trap"Flight disruptions affect 37% of annual connecting passengers, leading to cascading travel issues.Paperwork Fatigue: Travelers waste an average of 3-5 hours dealing with physical paperwork at transit airports.Information Asymmetry: Passengers often face "compensation confusion" due to complex multi-airline journey rules.
Infrastructure Constraints: Limited WiFi and language barriers make it difficult to access booking info or understand rights in real-time.Key Pain Points (Research-Validated)Through user testing, the following impact scores (out of 10) were identified:Lack of Accessibility: 9.2 Complex Submission Process: 8.5 No Status Tracker: 8.0 💡 The Solution: Data-Driven DesignFlyEase simplifies the disruption workflow by allowing users to scan their boarding pass for instant situation assessment.
